How to reach campus from the Bath Spa train station?

1- By bus: Bus U1 goes from the train station to campus (the bus stop is on Dorchester street, next to the little Sainsbury's, from the train station, cross the road and walk to the left until the bus stop), and its last stop is at the main bus stop on campus. You can pay on the bus directly to the driver by cash or contactless card (ask for a return to uni).

2- By taxi: You can ask the driver to take you to the main bus stop on campus. Please keep in mind that there is another university in Bath (called Bath Spa university), so make sure to tell the driver you need to go to the "University of Bath". The fare should be approximately £7.

3- Walking: Walking is possible but beware that the way to campus is (steeply) uphill!

Once on campus, how to reach my office in 6W?

From the main bus stop/east car park

1- Take the stairs up to "the parade" (a pedestrian street crossing the campus).
2- Walk through the parade until 4West (a glass-fronted building housing a café by the entrance).
3- Enter 4West, and go downstairs the main staircase at the end.
4- Once downstairs, go to the end of the atrium, and exit the building through the glass door to the left (there is a button at the left of the door to unlock it).
5- Walk through the small passage way and go up the ramp at your left, which goes to 6 West.
6- Once inside, take the door to your left and then follow the corridor (take a door left when you can't go straight anymore), you'll soon find my office, 6W 1.25a on your left-hand side.
