<div id="contenu">

  <p>
  I am a reader at the <a href="http://www.bath.ac.uk/"> University of Bath</a>, where I am a member of
  the probability group <a href="http://www.bath.ac.uk/research/centres/probability-laboratory/"> Prob-L@B</a>.
  Between 2018 and 2021, I was an EPSRC postdoctoral fellow (title of the fellowship: "Random trees: analysis and applications").
  Between 2013 and 2016, I was a postdoc at Prob-L@B, as part of <a href="http://people.bath.ac.uk/maspm/">Peter M&ouml;rters</a>' EPSRC project <a href="http://people.bath.ac.uk/maspm/coll.html"> Emergence of Condensation in Stochastic Networks</a>.
  Between 2010 and 2013, I was a PhD student whithin the <a href="http://lmv.math.cnrs.fr/"> Laboratoire de Mathématiques de Versailles</a>
  under the supervision of <a href="http://chauvin.perso.math.cnrs.fr/"> Brigitte Chauvin</a> and <a href="http://danielegardy.github.io"> Dani&egrave;le Gardy</a>.
  </p>


  <p>
  <!-- <a  href="images/multi_urn.png"><img id="urn" src="images/multi_urn.png" alt="[trajectories of an urn]" align="right" width="22%"/></a> -->
  I am interested in:
  <ul id="talks">
    <li>branching processes, random trees</li>
    <li>reinforcement, P&oacute;lya urns and stochastic approximation</li>
    <li>random networks, preferential attachment</li>
    <li>statistical physics: zero-range model, preferential attachment</li>
  </ul>
  </p>

  <p>I have been serving as an Associate Editor of the <a href="http://www.appliedprobability.org/index.aspx?Group=home&Page=home">Applied Probability Trust</a> (since October 2020),
and of <a href="https://www.journals.elsevier.com/stochastic-processes-and-their-applications">Stochastic Processes and Their Applications</a> (since March 2022).
  </p>

  I wrote a general introduction to P&oacute;lya urns for the
  <a href="https://www.lms.ac.uk/publications/lms-newsletter">LMS Newsletter</a>
  (November 2020, page 24).
  The intended audience is mathematicians in all areas of maths, starting at postgraduate level:
  the non-official title is "Why do probabilists still study coloured balls being drawn from urns?"
  <a href="LMS_feature.pdf">[pdf]</a>
